03

Exceptions Manager

Agentic exception management for enterprise operations

Definition phase

An agentic application designed to help operations teams detect and investigate exceptions across data and processes, identify likely root causes, recommend resolution actions and track outcomes through to closure.

The product is being designed around a human-in-the-loop model: agents gather context and perform analysis, while operational teams retain control over decisions and actions.

Planned capabilities

  • Detect and prioritise operational exceptions
  • Investigate root cause using available data and context
  • Recommend resolution actions with supporting evidence
  • Track actions, ownership and outcomes
  • Learn from resolved exceptions and human feedback

Current phase — defining user needs, exception workflows, agent responsibilities, success criteria and the technical approach.

  • LLMs
  • Workflow Automation
  • Human-in-the-loop
